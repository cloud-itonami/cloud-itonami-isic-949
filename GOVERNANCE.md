# Governance

## Decision-Making

This actor is governed by the cloud-itonami fleet's architecture and safety boundaries.

All proposals are subject to three HARD, un-overridable checks:
1. Member/record verification
2. Effect requirement (:propose only)
3. Scope exclusion (no membership-eligibility, religious doctrine, political position, dues-waiver, or disciplinary decisions)

## Escalation Path

Any proposal that fails governance checks or involves safety concerns is escalated to human review.
No overrides. No exceptions.

## Changes to Blueprint

Changes to the actor's scope, operations, or hard checks require ADR (Architecture Decision Record) in the superproject:
https://github.com/com-junkawasaki/root/90-docs/adr/
